Jump straight to the right page

Pick your app. We copy the URL when you open its settings, so you can paste it right away.

  1. Go to Settings → Apps → Advanced settings and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Back in Apps, click Create, name it Sakkhi and paste the URL.
  3. Choose OAuth, continue with Google, then pick Sakkhi from the tools menu in a new chat.

Needs a plan with developer mode. On workspace plans an admin may have to allow custom apps first. OpenAI moves these menus around, so if you don't see Apps, look under Connectors.

Copy URL & open ChatGPT settings
  1. Open Settings → Connectors and click Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Sakkhi, paste the URL and click Add.
  3. Continue with Google, then turn Sakkhi on from the tools menu in a chat.

Works on claude.ai, Claude Desktop and the mobile apps once added. Free plans allow one custom connector. On Team and Enterprise, an owner adds it for the organization first.

Copy URL & open Claude connectors
  1. Open Connected Apps in Gemini on the web.
  2. Under Custom apps, choose Add a custom app and paste the URL.
  3. Click Next, continue with Google and approve access.

Google is rolling custom apps out gradually. For now they need a personal Google account with Keep Activity on, may not be available in every country or plan, and can only be added on the web, not in the mobile app.

You decide what your AI can see.

Sakkhi Connect is read-only. It can't create, edit or delete your shoots or payments, and it only returns your own records. You pick the permissions on the consent screen.

  • Verified against your Sakkhi account with Google sign-in.
  • Read-only. No changes can be made through AI.
  • Disconnect any time from your AI app's settings.

Your AI provider receives whatever the tools you authorize return, under its own privacy settings and policies. Review permissions before you connect.

Before you connect.

Do I need a separate Sakkhi account?

No. Sign in with the Google account you already use for Sakkhi.

Can the AI book or change shoots for me?

No. Sakkhi Connect currently offers read-only tools. It can't modify your shoots or payments.

Why does the URL show 401 in my browser?

That's expected. The endpoint is protected, so opening it directly shows an error. Start the connection from your AI app instead.

Does every AI app support this?

No. The app needs to support remote MCP connectors and OAuth. Availability can also depend on your plan or organization settings.
