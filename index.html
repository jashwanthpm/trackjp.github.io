Frequently Asked Questions

Is my financial data secure?

Yes. Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL) with strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies. Only you can view your authenticated database records. We also feature an in-app "Privacy PIN" to blur amounts if you are reviewing schedules in public.

Does Sakkhi process payments directly?

No. We do not take a cut of your earnings. Sakkhi is a ledger and communication tool. Your WhatsApp prompts will include your own direct UPI or Bank details so clients pay you directly with zero transaction fees.

Can I export my data if I decide to leave?

Absolutely. You own your data. You can export your entire database to a standard CSV spreadsheet or a formatted PDF document at any time from the Account Data settings.
