Get connected

Connect in a few steps.

Use the custom MCP connector feature in a compatible AI app, where supported by your account and plan.

https://mcp.sakkhi.co.in/mcp
01 — Open your AI app

In ChatGPT, Claude, or another remote-MCP-compatible app, find its custom connector/integration settings. Availability and menu names vary by app and plan.

02 — Add Sakkhi

Create a connector using the URL above. Don't paste it into your browser address bar; the AI app needs to start the connection.

03 — Continue with Google

Review the Sakkhi consent screen and sign in using the same Google account you already use for Sakkhi. Only approve a connection you initiated.

04 — Ask away

Return to your AI conversation, enable the connector if your app requires it, and ask one of the sample questions above.

Good to know

Questions before you connect?

Do I need a separate Sakkhi account?

No. Sign in with the Google account already used for your Sakkhi account.

Can the AI book or change shoots for me?

No. Sakkhi Connect currently provides read-only tools; it cannot modify your records.

Why does the URL show 401 in my browser?

That's expected when opening the protected MCP endpoint without authentication. Start the connection from your AI app instead.

Does every AI app support this?

No. The app needs to support remote MCP connectors and OAuth. Feature availability may depend on your plan or organization settings.
